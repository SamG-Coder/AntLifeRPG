WORKER 041 · FIELD NOTES

A life beneath the ordinary.

Walk through the root arch to the communal store. The amber scent leads onward to an excavation crew. At the earth face, press Q to loosen a clump, E to lift it, and E at the spoil bed to deposit it.

Meet a nearby worker with G, or E when prompted. Share repeated days and deliveries to build trust. Eat at the store with E. Rest at your leaf bed with R. Follow the green trail to the surface and gather seeds. Press L on the ground with empty mandibles for an eight-second antenna grooming break. Moving or interacting interrupts it; dusty excavation lowers cleanliness.

C engages surface grip. While gripping, W/S advance and A/D turn; V toggles steady advance. C releases on the ground. Return to the ground and release before other interactions. Move with WASD. Hold Shift to hurry. Drag to orbit or use arrow keys to look. F changes perspective. Your colony saves automatically in this browser.
